# eDEX-UI BK — icon concept v2

## Koncepcja

Centralnym znakiem jest autorski, geometryczny monogram „E” zbudowany z trzech grubych szyn przypominających ścieżki układu GRID. Dolna szyna przechodzi w linię polecenia zakończoną odseparowanym blokowym kursorem, dzięki czemu ikona łączy markę eDEX z terminalem i pozostaje czytelna nawet przy 16–32 px; subtelna siatka oraz ciasny cyjanowy glow wiążą ją z interfejsem aplikacji bez powtarzania obecnych corner brackets.

## Final image-generation prompt

Create a single finished macOS application icon for a futuristic terminal app called eDEX-UI BK. Output a 2048 × 2048 px square image, viewed perfectly straight-on, with one centered macOS-style squircle and a truly transparent exterior outside the squircle if transparency is supported.

Inside the squircle, use an almost-black background: base color `#02080A`, deepening toward `#000000` near the outer edge. Add an extremely subtle large-cell technical grid in `#087F9C` at approximately 8–10% opacity. The grid is secondary atmosphere only: it may be visible at 256 px and larger, but it must disappear cleanly rather than become noise at 16–32 px. Add a restrained inner vignette and a very faint cyan illumination behind the central emblem. Keep the background clean and uncluttered.

The central emblem must be a bold custom geometric capital “E”, not ordinary font typography. Construct it from three thick horizontal circuit-like rails joined by one strong vertical spine on the left. Use uniform, substantial strokes with square or very slightly chamfered ends. The silhouette must read instantly as an “E” when reduced to 16 px. Make the emblem occupy roughly 62–66% of the squircle width and 58–62% of its height, with generous optical breathing room on all sides.

Transform the lower horizontal rail into a terminal command line: extend it slightly farther to the right and place one separate upright block cursor just beyond its endpoint, with a clear narrow gap between the rail and cursor. The cursor must be a simple vertical rectangle large enough to survive at 16 px. This should make the symbol read as “E + active terminal cursor” without using any additional characters, words, prompt symbols, or UI chrome.

Use `#00E5FF` as the main cyan of the emblem. Add a narrow `#8FF8FF` highlight along selected inner edges to create a crisp energized core, while using `#087F9C` only for subtle recessed circuit depth. Apply a tight, controlled glow close to the emblem and cursor: sharp luminous edges first, then a very weak ambient cyan bloom. The glow must never blur the silhouette or fill the negative spaces of the “E”. Aim for the softened Tron: Legacy / sci-fi GRID mood of a premium terminal HUD, not raw movie replication and not an exaggerated neon sign.

The squircle should feel like a polished native macOS icon but remain predominantly flat: a nearly black matte surface, an optional hairline inner rim in dim cyan at low opacity, and only minimal depth from restrained shading. Keep the composition symmetrical in visual weight, optically centered, bold, and memorable. Prioritize the emblem over decorative detail.

Critical small-size requirement: when viewed at 16 × 16 px or 32 × 32 px, the result must still show a clean cyan “E” silhouette and one distinct block cursor on a dark background. No essential information may depend on thin lines, tiny nodes, micro-circuit traces, texture, or glow.

Do not include any written words, app name, initials other than the single custom “E” emblem, numbers, terminal text, corner brackets, window frames, screenshots, keyboards, computers, faces, landscapes, lens flares, particles, glossy glass effects, rainbow colors, purple or blue gradients, heavy 3D extrusion, metallic chrome, perspective tilt, mockup presentation, drop-shadow outside the icon, or a surrounding scene. Produce only the final isolated app icon artwork.
